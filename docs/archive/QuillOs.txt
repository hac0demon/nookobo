How to Borrow the Best from Quill-OS

https://github.com/Quill-OS/quill

While you shouldn't use Quill-OS wholesale, its repositories are an excellent reference for:

    Power Management Scripts: Look at how InkBox handles CPU frequency scaling (scaling_governor = powersave) and deep sleep:
    Bash

    # InkBox-style sleep trigger for i.MX6SL
    echo mem > /sys/power/state

    FBInk Integration: Both Quill-OS and KOReader rely on NiLuJe's FBInk library. Because your Nook kernel exposes /dev/fb0 with NXP/Netronix ioctls, standard FBInk tools can draw directly to the screen, trigger waveform flashes, and clear ghosting outside of Android.

If your primary objective is a bloat-free reader that runs KOReader with 1 GB of RAM and proper sleep states, setting up an Alpine Linux rootfs alongside your stock boot kernel via TWRP remains the fastest and most stable path.
